Methodology

Definitions, data handling and backtest design

Definitions

Game state. The scoreline from a team’s point of view at the moment of an event: leading, level or trailing. Minutes in each state are measured from the minutes goals were scored. Match length is taken as the later of 97 minutes and the last recorded event.

Supremacy. The expected goal difference (home minus away) implied by Pinnacle’s pre-match Asian handicap and goal lines. Expected goals and supremacy are solved together from the two lines with a root-finder. Team supremacy is that value from the team’s own side (flipped for away teams).

Repeater. A corner by the same team, in the same half, within one minute of that team’s previous corner. Repeaters are given a value of 0.

Adjusted corner value. For a corner won by side s (home/away) in state g at team supremacy x:

\text{value} = \frac{\bar r_s}{\alpha_{s,g} + \beta_{s,g}\,x}

where \bar r_s is the league’s average corner rate for side s, and (\alpha, \beta) come from a line fitted to corners-per-minute across 30 supremacy buckets for that league, side and state. In the full model the fit is weighted by minutes in each bucket, and closed-doors COVID matches are excluded from fitting.

Ratings and prediction

  • Each team has an attack rating (corners won) and a defence rating (corners conceded): rolling means over its last N matches (12, 15, 19, 25 or 30), using only matches played before the fixture being predicted.
  • Four metrics are compared: raw counts, raw without repeaters (nr), adjusted values (adj), and adjusted without repeaters (adj_nr).
  • Home corners are predicted with OLS on the home attack rating, the away defence rating and match supremacy. Away corners are predicted the same way, the other way round.
  • Promoted and relegated teams keep their ratings across divisions. Supremacy in the new league’s fixtures accounts for the change in opposition.

Betting backtest

  • Test period: 2024/25 and 2025/26. The OLS model is fitted only on earlier seasons, and predictions are rescaled to the test period’s league corner level.
  • Market: Pinnacle Asian corner handicap. Its implied corner supremacy is compared with the model’s predicted home-minus-away corners.
  • Selection: a 1-unit bet on the side the model prefers when the two differ by 0.5 corners or more (a threshold sweep is shown in the analysis).
  • Settlement: actual raw corners at Pinnacle’s quoted odds, including quarter-line half-win and half-loss handling and pushes on whole lines.
  • Totals: corner over/under was backtested with the same set-up and lost money in every configuration. It’s no longer part of the live model.

Data caveats

  • Match and half lengths aren’t in the source data, so minutes in each state are estimates.
  • Some lower-league clubs appear under several spellings across seasons, which splits their rolling windows. This hasn’t been fixed at source yet.
  • Colombia Primera A/B and England League Two are excluded until they are given league/tier context.
  • The number of matches with odds available varies by league, and so does the ROI. Per-league results are noisy, and the combined figures are the ones to rely on.
